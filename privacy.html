Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

MailGuard ("we", "our", or "the application") is an email security and phishing detection system. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

1. Information We Access and Collect

When you connect your Google Account with MailGuard, we request read-only access (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly) to:

  • Retrieve recent email metadata (sender, subject line, date, and message body snippets) strictly for real-time security scanning.
  • Basic account profile information (email address and profile name) to authenticate your session.

2. How We Use Information

We use your information exclusively to:

  • Analyze email headers and content for phishing indicators, deceptive links, brand impersonation, and fraudulent wording.
  • Display email threat analytics (safe, spam, or fraud warnings) on your private dashboard.
  • Allow you to review quarantined or flagged emails.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

MailGuard's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your Google email data to third parties.
  • We do NOT use your email data for personalized advertising, marketing, or behavioral tracking.
  • We do NOT use your email data to train generalized AI/ML foundation models.
  • Humans do not read your email content; all analysis is automated via programmatic heuristic detectors.

4. Data Storage and Retention

Scanned threat records and security verdicts are saved securely in your account's private SQLite database. Your OAuth tokens are stored using industry-standard AES encryption. You can disconnect your account or purge your scan history at any time using the "Disconnect" button in the application.
